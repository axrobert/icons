# AXR Icon Design System V2

30 original, distinct mascots are present as initial hand-authored editable vector artwork.

## Invariants

- SVG viewBox 0 0 96 96.
- Background square 94 by 94, positioned at 1,1 and corner radius 23.
- Clear white mascot occupying most of x=20..76 and y=17..76, with dark small face details where appropriate.
- Colorful per-technology gradients, minimal shading and subtle highlights. No chunky 3D, no blur or purple blanket UI.
- No reuse of a shape merely recolored or rebranded.
- No corporate-logo copying or pixel tracing of the moodboard, no external images or fonts, no script.
- Review in gallery at 24px, 32px and 64px. CI only checks safety/uniqueness, not artistic quality.
